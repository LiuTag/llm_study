Mira found a blue stone beside the river. She carried it home and showed it to her brother. They thought it might be a jewel, but their mother said it was ordinary glass. Mira still kept it on her desk because it reminded her of the sunny walk.<|endoftext|>
Leo wanted to bake bread for his friends. He mixed flour, water, and a little salt, then waited while the dough grew. The first loaf was too dark, but the second smelled wonderful. Everyone shared a warm slice and asked Leo to bake again.<|endoftext|>
A small fox watched the snow fall outside its den. When the clouds cleared, it followed tiny footprints to a quiet pond. A bird was trying to find a dry place to rest. The fox pointed to a tree, and the bird sang a short song in thanks.<|endoftext|>
Nina planted three seeds in a pot by the window. Every morning she checked the soil and gave it a little water. One day a green leaf appeared. She called her grandfather to see it, and together they made room for the growing plant.<|endoftext|>
Owen lost his red kite in a strong wind. He searched the field and the path behind his house. At last he saw it caught in a low tree. His sister held the branches aside while Owen reached for the string, and they walked home together.<|endoftext|>
The village library opened early on Saturday. Ada chose a book about stars and found a chair near the window. When rain began, she read the story aloud to two younger children. They stayed until the sky was clear and borrowed more books to take home.<|endoftext|>
Sam built a little boat from a piece of wood and a paper sail. He put it in a shallow stream and ran beside it. The boat stopped at a stone, so he moved the stone carefully. Soon the boat sailed on, and Sam laughed all the way to the bridge.<|endoftext|>
Rosa heard a soft sound from the garden after dinner. Under a bush she found a wet kitten. She brought it a towel and some water, then asked the neighbors if it belonged to them. The kitten found its home before bedtime, and Rosa was glad she had listened.<|endoftext|>
